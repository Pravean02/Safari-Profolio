Safari: An AI-Driven Sri Lankan Tour Guide

Welcome to Safari, Sri Lanka's most advanced AI tour guide booking platform. Find trusted guides, plan your trip with intelligent recommendations, and enjoy a seamless digital experience.

About The Project

Our Vision

To revolutionize the Sri Lankan tourism landscape by connecting international tourists with local, verified guides—through secure, context-aware, AI-driven digital experiences.

Institutional Support

This research is conducted at SLIIT (Malabe), applying academic excellence to solve real-world problems in tourism, safety, and intelligent service matching.

Safari is built for trust, personalization, privacy, and effortless trip planning—every step of the journey.

Research Domain

Background

Sri Lanka’s tourism is rebounding fast—but travelers still struggle to find guides they can truly trust. Safari modernizes the experience with AI-powered trip planning and safer, verified guide discovery.

Research Gap

Most marketplaces rely on basic filters and reviews. They don’t understand user intent, context, or safety needs. Safari fills this gap with intelligent matching, identity verification, and privacy-aware communication.

Problem

Unverified profiles, weak screening, and uncontrolled information sharing create risk for tourists and guides. A smarter, secure platform is needed to reduce fraud, protect privacy, and increase confidence in bookings.

Objectives

Build an AI-driven booking platform that delivers: (1) accurate guide matching, (2) multi-layer verification, and (3) controlled information sharing—so every booking feels safe, professional, and personalized.

Research Features

Conversational AI Agent

Plan faster with a Gemini-powered assistant that understands your travel style, budget, and timing—then recommends itineraries and matches you with the right guide in seconds.

Privacy-Aware Workflow

We protect sensitive details by default. Contact info and personal data stay locked until a booking is confirmed—reducing scams, harassment, and misuse of user information.

Multi-Layered Verification

Trust is non-negotiable. Guides are verified through a three-step KYC pipeline (NIC + license + police check) to ensure authenticity and tourist safety.

Tools & Technologies

Frontend

Next.js + TypeScript for a fast, structured UI.
Tailwind CSS for clean, responsive design—optimized for mobile-first travelers.

Backend

Python + Flask to build scalable APIs.
Firebase for auth and real-time data.
Redis for efficient session and workflow handling.

AI & Integrations

Google Gemini as the LLM core.
RAG to ground answers in reliable data.
Stripe for secure payments and smooth checkout.
