About The Project

Our Vision

To revolutionize the Sri Lankan tourism landscape by connecting international tourists and local, thoroughly vetted guides through secure, context-aware, and intelligent digital experiences.

Institutional Support

This research is conducted at the Sri Lanka Institute of Information Technology (SLIIT), Malabe campus, leveraging academic excellence to solve real-world challenges in tourism.

Safari Project is dedicated to safety, personalization, privacy, and seamless tour planning—for every traveler.

Research Domain

Background

Tourism plays a critical role in Sri Lanka's economy. Travelers seek unique, safe, and authentic experiences through online platforms like Safari.

Research Gap

Existing platforms lack dynamic personalization and robust verification—Safari addresses these with AI and strong privacy controls.

Problem

Most platforms fail at identity verification, risk management, and flexible user need-matching—putting both tourists and guides at risk.

Objectives

To create an AI-powered platform for Sri Lanka that raises trust, privacy, and matching accuracy for everyone with robust verification and tailored digital tools.

Research Features

Conversational AI Agent

Get smart, instant itinerary recommendations—by chat or voice—powered by Google Gemini.

Privacy-Aware Workflow

Details are locked until a booking is official, protecting traveler and guide privacy at every step.

Multi-Layered Verification

Guides must pass three-step KYC: ID check, guide license, and police clearance for maximum trust.

Tools & Technologies

Frontend

Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS for blazing-fast, accessible, scalable web.

Backend

Python, Flask, Firebase, Redis—modern, modular APIs for reliable data and authentication.

AI & Integrations

Google Gemini (LLM), RAG, and Stripe API deliver intelligence and seamless transactions.
